How it works

From connecting a wallet to a family goal that fills itself.

01

Connect a wallet

Phantom, Solflare, whatever you already have. This is the only thing resembling a login, and it's just a wallet you control.

02

Create a goal, register your passkey

Name it, set a USDC target. Your browser prompts for Face ID or a fingerprint once, that's your approval key from here on.

03

Invite a family member

Share a one-time link. They connect their own wallet and register their own passkey on their own device, no key ever leaves either phone.

04

Contribute, approve, watch it fill

Either of you can add USDC anytime. Every contribution and withdrawal is approved with a face scan or fingerprint, and the balance comes straight from the chain.

Why trust it

Checkable claims, not reassurance copy.

Payments run on a live mainnet engine
Invoicing and swaps already move real USDC on Solana mainnet today, Jito bundles plus a standard RPC sent at the same time, the same engine goals and allowances use.
The on-chain program is public
Goals, allowances and passkey approvals run through a deployed Solana program you can inspect yourself: view it on Solana Explorer (devnet, where goals currently run).
Passkeys, not seed phrases
Approvals use your device's own secure hardware (the same system behind Face ID and fingerprint unlock), not a 12-word phrase anyone could copy down or lose.
No custody, ever
Sela never holds your funds or your keys. Every transaction is signed on your own device and settles directly on Solana.
